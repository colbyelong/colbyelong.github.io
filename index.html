In my research, I use tools from algebraic geometry, algebraic statistics, and discrete mathematics to address questions in phylogenetics. Before starting at The College of Wooster, I received my PhD in Mathematics from North Carolina State University and was a postdoctoral research fellow at the Mathematical Biosciences Institute at The Ohio State University.
